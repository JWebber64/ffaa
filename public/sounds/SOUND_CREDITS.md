# FFAA Sound Credits

Most WAV files in this folder are original procedural sound effects generated for this project. They do not include third-party samples, copied media, or downloaded soundboard clips.

`sold.mp3` is "Gavel, 1 blow" by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, released as CC0 public domain. Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/gavel-1-blow-s1588.html

Use downloaded sounds only when the source page has a clear license. Prefer CC0 or no-attribution commercial-use sources such as Kenney, Pixabay, Mixkit, Freesound CC0, or OpenGameArt CC0. If an attribution license is used, add the title, creator, source URL, and license here before committing the asset.

Optional generated auctioneer voice clips live under `public/sounds/voice/` when created. Generated AI voice clips must include their own `SOUND_CREDITS.md` and the app should disclose to users that the voice is AI-generated.
